Walking Ann Arbor & U-M

A big loop through downtown and central campus for Will's visit.

Suggested stops

4.1 miles, about 2½–3 hours with stops. Starts and ends at Main & William.

  1. 1Start: S Main & E William.
  2. 2Main Street. Downtown is the more grown-up part of Ann Arbor. I'd think of it as where you and Caitlin would come if Will ended up going here, or where you'd go if you wanted to take Will out somewhere nicer than a typical college-town restaurant.
  3. 3Zingerman's Deli · 422 Detroit St. The classic Ann Arbor deli. If College GameDay is ever in Ann Arbor, you'll see a shot of Zingerman's.
  4. 4East Liberty Street. The more student-feeling stretch of downtown.
  5. 5Modern Languages Building. A good example of a large lecture hall. This is the kind of classroom where Will might take an intro course like Intro to Psychology.
  6. 6Burton Memorial Tower. The classic college bell tower.
  7. 7Michigan League (optional). Worth poking your head in if it's open, though I don't really know what the League does or is for.
  8. 8Hill Auditorium. Will will have his freshman welcome here. Peek your head in if it's open.
  9. 9The Diag. This is the block M they'll show on College GameDay. You're not supposed to step on it until you've taken your first exam.
  10. 10Angell Hall & the Fishbowl. Take a look inside. For lack of a better term, it's the heart of LSA, or at least it's representative of the LSA experience.
  11. 11Shapiro Library & Hatcher. If seeing a library is important to Will, check out Shapiro. It's called the UGLi. You can also check out Hatcher, which has the stacks, which I feel like is a quintessential college thing.
  12. 12Engineering Arch. Leave the Diag through the arch under West Hall.
  13. 13East Quad. East Quad has a weird residential program. Unless Will has a very specific vision of never leaving his dorm his freshman year, I wouldn't recommend it.
  14. 14Ross School of Business. The new Ross building, which didn't exist when I was there, is a much, much nicer version of the business school I went to. Note the name of the street.
  15. 15Law Quad & Law Library Reading Room. Go look at the Law Library Reading Room if somebody will let you in. Tell Will he can pretend he's Harry Potter when he studies there.
  16. 16Michigan Union. I don't really know what goes on in the Union, but they do have a bunch of food options in the basement.
  17. 17The Cube. For some unknown reason, this is a Michigan landmark. Go spin it around.
  18. 18South State Street · Rally House, 303 S State St. Walk down South State Street. It borders the campus, with bars and restaurants, and it'll give you a good feel for what it's like walking around the university. You can buy some swag at Rally House.
  19. 19Back downtown. Frita Batidos is just past Main on W Washington if you're hungry.

Optional: Hill dorms

  • Palmer Field. The main freshman dorms are all around this field. If Will lives in the dorms freshman year, in all likelihood it'll be one of the ones up here on what's called the Hill.
  • Freshman dorms: Stockwell, Mosher-Jordan (MoJo), Alice Lloyd, Couzens, Markley

If you have time

  • Nichols Arboretum (the Arb). If it's a really nice day and you have time, it's right next to the Hill dorms, so it's easy to get to from there. You can walk all the way through to the Huron River if you like.
  • North Campus (engineering). If Will is considering engineering, you need to go up to North Campus. It's a drive up there from Central Campus; you couldn't walk there. Only important to see if Will is going to do engineering or music, I believe. Both are up there, though I don't know North Campus well.

Food

  • Pizza House · 618 Church St. Great undergrad food, a block off the East University leg.
  • Frita Batidos · 117 W Washington St. A newer place that's good. Cuban-inspired street food, downtown.
