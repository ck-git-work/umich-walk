Walking Ann Arbor & U-M

A big loop through downtown and central campus for Will's visit: the spots I'd show him myself.

The walk

Starts and ends downtown at Main & William. Follow the numbers.

  1. 1

    Start: S Main & E William

    Start here, downtown. Head north on Main Street.

    Walking directions ↗
  2. 2

    Main Street (turn at Kingsley)

    Main Street is the more grown-up side of Ann Arbor: restaurants, bars and shops. Walk north until Kingsley St, then turn right (east).

    Walking directions ↗
  3. 3

    Zingerman's Deli

    422 Detroit St

    A classic. If College GameDay is ever in Ann Arbor, you'll see a shot of Zingerman's. A great deli, and a good place to stop for a meal if you're hungry.

    Walking directions ↗
  4. 4

    East Liberty Street

    Head back south on Division St, then east on Liberty. E Liberty is the more student-feeling stretch of downtown. Turn left (north) on State St when you reach it.

    Walking directions ↗
  5. 5

    Burton Memorial Tower

    From State St, go right (east) on E Washington to Ingalls Mall. This is the bell tower.

    Walking directions ↗
  6. 6

    Michigan League (optional)

    Optional. Worth poking your head in if it's open.

    Walking directions ↗
  7. 7

    Hill Auditorium

    Cross the street after the tower. Peek in if it's open.

    Walking directions ↗
  8. 8

    The Diag

    Cross N University Ave and keep going down Ingalls Mall to the Diag, the heart of central campus. Look for the brass block "M" in the middle: students avoid stepping on it. Legend says if you step on it, you'll fail your first exam.

    Walking directions ↗
  9. 9

    Angell Hall & the Fishbowl

    Go into Angell Hall and see the Fishbowl computer lab. For lack of a better term, it's the heart of LSA (the College of Literature, Science, and the Arts).

    Walking directions ↗
  10. 10

    Shapiro Library (and Hatcher)

    If seeing the library is on the list: Shapiro is the undergrad library where students actually hang out. It's connected to Hatcher, the grand graduate library.

    Walking directions ↗
  11. 11

    Engineering Arch

    Leave the Diag through the arch under West Hall (southeast corner). It lets you out at S University & East University.

    Walking directions ↗
  12. 12

    East Quad

    Walk south on East University. East Quad has a weird residential program (the Residential College). I'd recommend Will NOT do it.

    Walking directions ↗
  13. 13

    Ross School of Business

    Right (west) on Hill St, then right (north) on Tappan. The new Ross building, which didn't exist when I was there.

    Walking directions ↗
  14. 14

    Law Quad & Law Library Reading Room

    Go diagonally across the street to the front of the Law Quad. Go look at the Law Library Reading Room if someone will let you in (access may be limited). Tell Will he can pretend he's Harry Potter by studying there.

    Walking directions ↗
  15. 15

    Michigan Union

    Exit the Law Quad onto State St. The Union has some food options.

    Walking directions ↗
  16. 16

    The Cube

    Officially "Endover," in Regents Plaza. For some unknown reason, a Michigan landmark. It spins if you push it.

    Walking directions ↗
  17. 17

    Rally House

    303 S State St

    Walk north on State St: the university on your right, shops on your left, and more shops past where campus ends. Rally House is where to buy some swag if Will is into it.

    Walking directions ↗
  18. 18

    Back downtown via Liberty

    Head west on Liberty back to Main Street. The start is one block south.

Optional: the Hill dorms

Where Will might live freshman year.

Best place to break off: right after Hill Auditorium (stop 7). Walk east up N University Ave, cross Washtenaw, and follow the path between Palmer Field and the dorms. Then come back the same way and pick up at the Diag (stop 8). Adds about 1.3 miles round trip (25–30 min).

  • Palmer Field

    The main freshman dorms are all around this field. This is where Will might live freshman year.

If you have time

  • Nichols Arboretum (the Arb)

    If it's a really nice day and you have time. It's right next to the Hill dorms, so it's an easy add-on to that detour: from Observatory St, walk east to the end of Washington Heights and you're at the edge of the Arb. The trails head down to the Huron River.

    Walking directions ↗

  • North Campus (engineering)

    If Will is considering engineering, you need to go up to North Campus. It's a drive or a U-M bus from Central Campus, not part of the walk. Worth seeing: Lurie Tower (this pin), the Duderstadt Center next door, and the Wave Field.

Good to know

  • Wear good shoes: it's close to 4 miles before any detours.
  • If it's a home football Saturday, everything will be packed. Plan food stops early.
  • Buildings (the League, Hill Auditorium, Angell Hall, the Law Library) may be locked on weekends or during events. Peek in if you can.
  • For the official campus tour and info session, book through U-M Admissions.
